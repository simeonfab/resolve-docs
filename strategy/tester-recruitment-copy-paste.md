Hi — thanks again for agreeing to test this! Here's everything you need.

**Link:** [INSERT LINK]

**Important context first:** this is a clickthrough mockup, not a working app yet. Buttons mostly just highlight or move you to the next screen — nothing actually processes, saves, or submits. That's expected, not a bug, so don't worry if clicking something doesn't "do" anything.

**What to do:**
Go through all six screens, in order, once. There's nothing to complete or submit at the end — just get to the last screen and you're done.

**Ideally — screen record + talk out loud the whole time** (this is genuinely the most useful format, so if you're able to, please do this one). On most phones/laptops there's a built-in screen recorder (iPhone: Control Centre → Screen Recording; Mac: Cmd+Shift+5; Windows: Win+G or Win+Alt+R). Just hit record, share your screen, and talk continuously as you go — you don't need to edit anything, just send me the raw file or a link to it after.

**If recording is too much hassle, written notes are totally fine instead** — just jot down your reactions screen by screen as you go (even rough/messy notes are useful, don't tidy them up for me).

**What to actually say/write, screen by screen — basically just think out loud:**
- What do you think this screen is asking you to do?
- What would you expect to happen if you clicked the obvious button/option here?
- Is anything confusing, unclear, or making you hesitate?
- Any reaction at all — even "I don't get this" or "this seems pointless" is exactly what I want to hear.

Then click through to the next screen and do the same thing again, all the way to screen six.

**At the end, a few quick questions (say/write these too):**
1. What did you think this tool was for, before you started?
2. Was there a screen where you genuinely didn't know what you were meant to do?
3. Now you've seen all six, what would you actually do with something like this if it were real — use it, forget it, tell someone else about it?
4. Anything else — confusing wording, things you liked, anything at all.

No need to be polite — blunt and critical is exactly what's useful. Thanks so much for doing this, genuinely appreciate it.
