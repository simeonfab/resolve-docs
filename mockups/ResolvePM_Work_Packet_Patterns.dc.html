Bhouse-wide · every narrated loader

What a loader does when the real wait outlasts its story.

No loader today says what happens past the end of its narrated loop — checklists just stall on the last tick. This is the generic overrun treatment, specced here against the intake "Reading your project" loader but written to drop onto any narrated loader (Weighing, cockpit milestones). Three moments: the loop's honest end, the softened overrun, and the boundary into failure.

After N seconds past the loop (spec: 8s) the copy softens and reframes the wait as depth, not a fault. The ring keeps spinning; an indeterminate shimmer replaces the finished checklist's progress so it never reads as frozen. The last line gains a quiet honest qualifier. Only past a hard ceiling (spec: 45s) does overrun hand to the existing failed sibling. This is the one place the tone shifts from breathe to stop: ring goes still + terracotta, copy owns the fault ("our side, not your project"), and a real retry appears. Everything before this stays warm.

The two thresholds

N = 8s past loop end → soften to overrun. 45s absolute → failure boundary. Both are per-loader overridable; these are the house defaults. Between them the loader is honest about waiting without ever implying it's broken.

Drop-in for any loader

Only two strings change per loader: the overrun headline and the softened subline. The shimmer, thresholds and failure hand-off are shared. Cockpit milestone loaders inherit it unchanged.

Banned in overrun

  • Countdowns or "~30s remaining"
  • A spinner that stops before failure (reads as frozen)
  • Apology tone before the 45s boundary
  • Blaming the user's input for the wait

Overdue is information, not shame: same chip, terracotta ink + a small dot, "overdue" spelled out — no red, no icon-alarm. Delete confirms inline on the row (drawer action mirror), then the undo toast is the real safety net. Done collapses to a count so completing reads as banked progress.

1 · Create

New task lives in the Now section header (quiet text button) and the global bar. Opens a bottom sheet: title required, description + due date optional. Adds straight to Now.

2 · Date picker

In-drawer month grid, clearable, today marked. A styled platform-native <input type=date> is an acceptable fallback on mobile — the custom grid is the drawer-consistent default.

4 · Complete

Fast path: tap the circle. Row shows a ~700ms settle (checkmark fills, fades to .5) then drops into Done — long enough to feel earned, short enough not to block.

5 · Delete + undo

Soft delete. Inline confirm on the row, then a ~5s undo toast with a draining amber bar. Undo restores in place. Nothing is hard-deleted until the toast expires.

Eroadmap "whole picture" · theme rail

Theme titles that stay whole.

The vertical theme rail on the roadmap grid is only ~64px wide, so long titles fragment mid-word, one syllable per line. Acceptance: no title broken mid-word or cut off, at 390 or desktop, up to ~60 characters. Three treatments below; the recommend is E3.

Recommended. Rail widens to 130px; titles wrap on word boundaries, clamp to 2 lines, and anything longer truncates with the full title on tap/hover (the same provisional-tooltip affordance already in the grid). Reads naturally, holds ~60 chars, identical at 390 and desktop.

Why E3

It keeps horizontal reading (fastest), never breaks a word (word-break stays normal), and reuses the existing tooltip for overflow — no new affordance to build. Costs ~66px of grid width vs E2, acceptable at 390 with 2 visible phase columns.

Acceptance test

Longest live theme "Reposition for a higher price point" (35 ch) wraps to 2 clean lines. A synthetic 60-char title clamps to 2 lines + ellipsis, full text on tap. No mid-word break at either width.

Fallback

If product wants maximum grid width for a dense portfolio view, E2 (rotated) is the drop-in alternate — same data, same tooltip, narrower rail.
